About

I'm a UX designer based in Monterey, California. I help small teams turn messy ideas into clear, friendly interfaces, and I sketch every project by hand before I ever open a design tool.

Outside of work, I volunteer at a local marine wildlife center, paint watercolors of the coastline, and kayak the bay on weekends, usually while counting sea otters.
